The corpus was created by the AMI Project and its contributors. bestASR uses a
SHA-256-pinned subset of public meeting audio and manual segment annotations for
local, reproducible speaker diarization and cross-session identity evaluation.
The downloaded audio and annotations are not bundled with bestASR and are not
stored in this Git repository. Prepared audio and derived speaker profiles remain
on local external storage.

Suggested corpus citation:

- Jean Carletta et al., “The AMI Meeting Corpus: A Pre-announcement,”
  Machine Learning for Multimodal Interaction, 2005.

bestASR does not modify the source corpus. Evaluation preparation decodes the
selected WAV signals to local Float32 PCM clips and records that transformation
in the local run manifest. No endorsement by the AMI Project, its institutions,
or its contributors is implied.
