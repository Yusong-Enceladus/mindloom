The Core ML conversion is published by Fluid Inference and is based on the
pyannote speaker-diarization-community-1 model. The model card identifies the
parent model and its CC BY 4.0 license. bestASR uses the pinned, unmodified
compiled model files for fully local speaker diarization and embedding.

Required citations retained from the model card:

- Alexis Plaquet and Hervé Bredin, “Powerset multi-class cross entropy loss
  for neural speaker diarization,” INTERSPEECH 2023.

  embedding learning toolkit,” ICASSP 2023.
- Federico Landini et al., “Bayesian HMM clustering of x-vector sequences
  (VBx) in speaker diarization: theory, implementation and analysis on
