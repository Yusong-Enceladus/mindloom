FLEURS was created by Google Research and its contributors. bestASR uses a
deterministically selected subset of the public Mandarin Chinese and US English
development splits for local ASR tuning and candidate comparison. Downloaded
audio and transcript references are not bundled with bestASR and are not stored
in this Git repository. The selected payload and content-bearing diagnostics
remain on local external storage.

Suggested corpus citation:

- Alexis Conneau et al., “FLEURS: Few-shot Learning Evaluation of Universal
  Representations of Speech,” 2022, arXiv:2205.12446.

bestASR does not attempt to identify any corpus speaker. Evaluation copies only
the selected WAV signals into an isolated local tuning root and records that
selection in a content-free manifest. No endorsement by Google or the corpus
contributors is implied.
